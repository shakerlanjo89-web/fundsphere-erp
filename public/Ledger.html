📌 Ledger Calculation:
Member Contributions are recorded as Credit.
Business Income and Profit are recorded as Credit.
Business Withdrawals are recorded as Debit.
Emergency Fund Income / Deposit is recorded as Credit.
Emergency Fund Withdrawals are recorded as Debit.
General Expenses are recorded as Debit.
Running Balance = Total Credit − Total Debit .

Note: Contribution allocations are not counted again as separate transactions in the ledger.
